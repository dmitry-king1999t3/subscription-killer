## Why

People often lose **$30–50 per month** on subscriptions they forgot about:

- Free trials that automatically renewed
- Duplicate services
- Unnoticed price increases
- Subscriptions they no longer use

**subscription-killer** helps you find recurring charges and identify potential savings.

## How it works

1. Import a bank statement in CSV format
2. Detect recurring charges by merchant, amount, and billing period
3. Identify abandoned subscriptions, duplicates, and price increases
4. Estimate how much you could save

## Features

- 🔒 **100% local** — your financial data stays on your computer
- 💸 **Find hidden expenses** — discover subscriptions you may no longer use
- ⚡ **Simple to use** — import a CSV and scan your transactions
- 🌍 **Multi-bank support** — compatible with supported bank CSV formats
- 🧩 **Extensible** — designed to support additional bank formats
